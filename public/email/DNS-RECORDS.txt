Conalytic email branding — add these DNS records in Cloudflare
================================================================

Prerequisites (Resend dashboard):
- Domain conalytic.com verified (SPF + DKIM green)

1) DMARC (required before Gmail shows BIMI logo)
Record type: TXT
Name: _dmarc
Content: v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; pct=100; rua=mailto:admin@conalytic.com; adkim=s; aspf=s

After 2–4 weeks of clean sending, upgrade to:
v=DMARC1; p=reject; pct=100; rua=mailto:admin@conalytic.com; adkim=s; aspf=s

2) BIMI logo record
Record type: TXT
Name: default._bimi
Content: v=BIMI1; l=https://conalytic.com/email/bimi-logo.svg;

Gmail also requires a certificate (VMC or CMC) in the record:
v=BIMI1; l=https://conalytic.com/email/bimi-logo.svg; a=https://conalytic.com/email/bimi-certificate.pem;

Obtain CMC/VMC from a BIMI provider (e.g. DigiCert, Entrust) after DMARC is at enforcement.

3) Hosted assets (deployed with the website)
- Logo PNG (512×512): https://conalytic.com/email/logo-512.png
- BIMI SVG: https://conalytic.com/email/bimi-logo.svg

4) Resend sender format
RESEND_FROM=Conalytic <admin@conalytic.com>

Logo in Gmail inbox list appears only after BIMI + DMARC enforcement + certificate (not from PNG alone).
